Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how ANCHOR Gym System (“ANCHOR,” “we,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our customer website and gym services.

Information we collect

  • Account and contact details: name, email address, phone number, and any profile photo you choose to upload.
  • Sign-in information: your password is stored as a cryptographic hash, not as readable text. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides us with your verified email address and, when available, your name. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Membership and gym records: membership applications, plan and status, QR/check-in details, attendance and visit records, and related customer service communications.
  • Payment records: payment method, amount, status, transaction/reference numbers, and any payment proof image you submit. ANCHOR does not ask you to enter your bank password, card PIN, or Google password in the customer website.
  • Technical and session information: information needed to keep the website and your sign-in session working, including session data stored in your browser.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and manage customer accounts and memberships;
  • process membership applications and record payments;
  • provide gym access, QR check-in, and attendance records;
  • authenticate customers and protect accounts;
  • send password-reset codes and respond to support requests; and
  • maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the service.

Google sign-in and password-reset email

If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares sign-in information with us so we can authenticate you and create or access your ANCHOR account. We use that information for sign-in and account management, not to build advertising profiles.

Password-reset codes are sent by email through the ANCHOR operator’s configured Gmail service. This sending service is used to deliver reset messages; ANCHOR does not use it to read your Gmail inbox.

When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Vercel, Render, and the configured database provider to host, store, secure, and operate the website, and by Google for sign-in or password-reset email delivery. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect customers, the gym, or the service.

Payments may involve an external payment provider. Its own privacy terms apply to information you provide directly to it.

Retention and security

We keep account, membership, attendance, and transaction information while it is needed to operate the gym, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. Retention periods can depend on the type of record. We use safeguards intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask to access or correct your account information, or to request deletion. Some membership, attendance, security, or transaction records may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You can stop using Google sign-in at any time. To manage or revoke Google account permissions, visit your Google Account’s third-party connections settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or services change. The effective date above indicates when this version was last updated.
